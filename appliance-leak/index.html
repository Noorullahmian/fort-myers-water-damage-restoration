Appliance Leak Water Damage in Fort Myers

Washing machine overflowed across the laundry room, a dishwasher leaking under the cabinets, or a puddle by the fridge? Appliance leak water damage looks small, but in Fort Myers slab homes the water runs under cabinets, baseboards and vinyl plank where you can't see it, and in a condo it can reach the unit below.

24/7 Help for Washer, Dishwasher and Fridge Leaks in Fort Myers

Shut off the appliance and its water valve first; for a washer, also stop the drain hose from overflowing.

Appliance water spreads under cabinets, toe kicks and flooring, so the wet area is usually bigger than the puddle.

Washer and dishwasher water is gray water, Category 2, and needs cleaning as well as drying.

Appliance leaking right now? Do this first

  1. Turn off the appliance and unplug it if you can reach the plug without standing in water. Otherwise, switch off its breaker.
  2. Close its water valve. Washers have hot and cold valves on the wall box. Dishwashers usually shut off under the kitchen sink. Refrigerator ice maker lines often have a small valve under the sink or behind the fridge.
  3. If you can't find the valve, shut off the main water to the house.
  4. Soak up standing water with towels or a wet/dry vac, and move rugs and belongings out of it.
  5. Photograph and video everything before cleanup, including the appliance, the hose or line that failed, and the floor.
  6. In a condo, let the unit below and building management know right away.
  7. Start drying the same day. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in Florida humidity.

Why appliance leaks cause more damage than they look

Water goes under things. Appliances sit next to cabinets and on top of flooring. Water runs under the toe kicks, beneath the cabinet floors and under vinyl plank or laminate, where it's trapped against the slab.

Leaks are often slow. An ice maker line or dishwasher connection can drip for weeks before anyone notices a soft cabinet floor, lifting plank or a musty smell.

Particle board swells. Most cabinet bases and many laundry shelves are particle board. Once it swells, it doesn't go back. See water damaged kitchen cabinets.

Condos stack laundry rooms. In multi-story buildings, a washer overflow can come through the ceiling below almost right away. See condo water damage.

Gray water contains detergent, food and soil, so carpet pad and porous materials it soaks are often removed, and surfaces are cleaned and treated before drying.

How appliance leak cleanup works

  1. Stop the source. An appliance technician or plumber repairs or replaces the hose, line or appliance.
  2. Extract standing water from carpet and floors with emergency water removal equipment.
  3. Pull the appliance out and check the wall, floor and cabinets around and behind it.
  4. Map the moisture under flooring, inside cabinet bases and up the walls with meters and a thermal camera.
  5. Remove what can't be dried, such as soaked carpet pad, swollen toe kicks, particle board cabinet floors and wet vinyl plank.
  6. Clean and treat gray-water areas with antimicrobial.
  7. Dry the structure with dehumidifiers and air movers, logging readings daily. See how structural drying works.
  8. Repair flooring, cabinets, baseboards and drywall once everything reads dry.

Most appliance leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days if drying starts quickly.

Floors after an appliance leak

Appliance water often ends up under the flooring. Tile that's still bonded can usually stay while the slab and grout are dried. Vinyl plank and laminate often have to be lifted, because they trap water against the concrete. See water damaged floors.

How to prevent the next appliance leak

  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided stainless hoses, and replace hoses every few years.
  • Leave space behind the washer so the hoses don't kink.
  • Turn off washer valves when you leave town.
  • Use a washer drain pan in condos and second-floor laundry rooms.
  • Check the ice maker line when you move the fridge, and consider braided or copper tubing.
  • Install leak alarms or automatic shut-off valves under sinks, behind the fridge and next to the washer.
  • Don't run the washer or dishwasher while nobody is home.

Does insurance cover appliance leak water damage?

  • Sudden leaks, such as a burst washer hose, are often covered for the resulting damage.
  • The appliance or the failed hose itself usually isn't.
  • Slow leaks that ran for weeks may be excluded as continuous seepage.
  • Condos: damage to the unit below can involve your HO-6, their policy and the association.

Questions about appliance leaks

My washing machine overflowed. What should I do first?

Turn the washer off, close the hot and cold valves behind it, and pull the drain hose out of the standpipe if it's overflowing. Soak up the water, photograph everything and check the walls and floors in nearby rooms.

Can a dishwasher leak damage the floor under the cabinets?

Yes. Dishwasher water runs under the cabinets and toe kicks and soaks particle board cabinet floors and the flooring beneath. Pull the toe kick and check with a moisture meter.

How do I know if my refrigerator is leaking under the floor?

Look for a puddle that keeps returning, lifting or soft vinyl plank, stained grout, or a musty smell near the fridge. A cracked ice maker line can leak slowly for weeks before water shows.

Is washing machine water dangerous?

Drain water from a washer is gray water, Category 2. It isn't sewage, but it contains detergent, soil and bacteria, so wet carpet pad and porous materials are usually removed and the area is cleaned before drying.

My washer leaked into the condo below. Who pays?

It depends on your condo documents, the cause and your policies. Report it to the association and your insurer right away. See condo water damage.

How long does it take to dry after an appliance leak?

Most appliance leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days with commercial equipment. Leaks that soaked cabinets or ran under vinyl plank take longer.

Should I keep using the washer after it leaked?

No. Leave it off with the valves closed until the hose, drain or machine has been checked. Running it again can push more water under the floor.

Can a refrigerator leak cause mold?

Yes. A slow ice maker or defrost drain leak can keep the floor and cabinets damp for weeks, which is enough for mold in Florida humidity. Pull the fridge out and check behind and under it.

Appliance leak in your home?

Shut off the appliance and its water, take photos and call. Finding the hidden water early saves cabinets and floors.
