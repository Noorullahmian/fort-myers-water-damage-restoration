Water damage in North Fort Myers right now? Do this first

  1. Shut off the water at the fixture or the main. In a manufactured home, the main shut-off is often under the home near where the supply line enters.
  2. Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets or appliances.
  3. Turn off the water heater if it's leaking, at its breaker or gas control.
  4. Photograph everything before cleanup, including under the home if it's safe.
  5. Start drying the same day. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in Florida humidity.

Manufactured homes: where the water goes

In a manufactured home, water from a leak usually ends up in one of three places:

  • The floor decking, which swells and goes soft, often near the water heater, toilet, kitchen sink or washer.
  • The insulation in the floor system, which holds water against the decking and framing.
  • The belly wrap, the bottom board under the home, which can fill with water and sag.

Drying a manufactured home often means opening the belly wrap, removing wet insulation and replacing damaged decking, not just running fans inside.

Flooding along the river

When rising water reaches a home, it's floodwater, Category 3, even if it looks clear. Wet drywall, insulation, carpet and particle board usually have to be removed before drying. In manufactured homes, floodwater fills the belly and soaks the floor system from below. Flood damage is covered by a flood policy, not standard homeowners insurance.

How water damage restoration works in North Fort Myers homes

  1. Stop the source. A plumber, roofer or HVAC technician fixes the cause.
  2. Extract standing water with emergency water removal equipment.
  3. Map the moisture in floors, walls and, in manufactured homes, the floor system and belly.
  4. Remove what can't be dried, such as soaked carpet pad, swollen decking, wet insulation and baseboards.
  5. Dry the structure with dehumidifiers and air movers, logging readings daily. See how structural drying works.
  6. Repair once everything reads dry.

Riverfront and canal homes

Homes along the Caloosahatchee, its creeks and the canals north of the river face surge during hurricanes and high water during heavy rain. When floodwater reaches the house, drywall is usually cut above the wet line, wet insulation is removed and the slab and framing are disinfected before drying. Garages and lanais often take water first. Check your flood zone and flood coverage before storm season, since new flood policies usually have a 30-day waiting period.

Water heaters and older homes

Many North Fort Myers homes have water heaters in closets, utility rooms or garages, and in manufactured homes they're often in a small closet next to the bathroom or kitchen. A tank that's 10 or more years old is a common source of floor damage. If you see rust, moisture around the base or a soft floor next to the heater, have it checked before it fails. See water heater leak cleanup.

Seasonal and 55+ communities

North Fort Myers has many seasonal and 55+ residents. Before leaving for the summer, shut off the main water, set the AC to control humidity and arrange for someone to check the home regularly. After a storm, have someone look at the home as soon as it's safe, even if you're away.

My mobile home floor is soft near the water heater. What does that mean?

Usually that water has been leaking into the floor decking. Shut off the heater and the water, and have the floor and the belly underneath checked for moisture.

Do you work in manufactured-home and 55+ communities?

Yes. Let your community office know about the work if they require it, and have your lot number ready when you call.

Is river flooding covered by homeowners insurance?

No. Rising water from the river or storm surge is flood damage, covered by a flood policy.

How long does drying take in a manufactured home?

Often a few days for a clean-water leak caught early, but longer when the belly wrap and insulation are wet or decking has to be replaced.

Do you also cover Cape Coral and Fort Myers?

Yes. Help is available across Lee County. See water damage restoration in Cape Coral and all service areas.

Does insurance cover a soft mobile home floor?

Damage from a sudden leak is often covered; damage from a slow leak over months often isn't. Photograph the floor and the source, and ask your insurer.

Should I shut off the water when I leave for the summer?

Yes. Shutting off the main water and turning off the water heater is one of the simplest ways to prevent a leak from running for months.
