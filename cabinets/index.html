Water Damaged Kitchen Cabinets in Fort Myers

Swollen cabinet bottoms, a soft sink base or a musty smell every time you open the cabinet under the sink? Water damaged kitchen cabinets usually hide a bigger problem: water trapped behind the toe kick and under the cabinet floor, sometimes for weeks. Dried early, many cabinets and countertops can be saved.

24/7 Help for Water-Damaged Cabinets in Fort Myers Homes

  • Water from sinks, dishwashers and fridge lines runs under cabinets and behind toe kicks, where you can't see it.
  • Particle board cabinet floors swell and don't recover; solid wood and plywood can often be dried in place.
  • Drying behind and under cabinets often saves the countertops and the cabinets themselves.

Water under your cabinets right now? Do this first

  1. Shut off the water at the valves under the sink, or the main if you can't find the source.
  2. Empty the cabinet and photograph everything inside before cleanup.
  3. Soak up standing water and leave the doors open.
  4. Turn off the dishwasher or disposal at the breaker if they're near the water.
  5. Photograph the cabinet floor, toe kick and nearby flooring.
  6. Start drying the same day. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in Florida humidity.

Many cabinet sink bases can be repaired by replacing just the cabinet floor panel, rather than the whole cabinet, if the rest is sound.

Why water hides under cabinets

Cabinets sit on a base or toe kick, with an enclosed space between the cabinet floor and the slab. When water gets under there, it's trapped with no airflow. It soaks the bottom of the drywall behind the cabinets, the flooring under the toe kick and the cabinet floor itself. In Southwest Florida humidity, that hidden space is a common place for mold to grow.

How water-damaged cabinets are dried

  1. Stop the source. A plumber or appliance technician fixes the leak.
  2. Empty the cabinets and remove the toe kicks.
  3. Map the moisture in the cabinet floors, the wall behind and the flooring underneath with meters and a thermal camera.
  4. Drill small holes or open the toe kick space so dry air can be pushed underneath and behind the cabinets.
  5. Remove what can't be dried, such as swollen particle board panels and wet drywall behind the cabinets if needed.
  6. Dry the space with dehumidifiers and air movers, logging readings daily. See how structural drying works.
  7. Replace damaged panels, toe kicks and drywall once everything reads dry.

Done early, this often saves the cabinets and countertop. When a leak has sat for weeks or the cabinets are badly swollen, removal and replacement is often the better choice.

When countertops have to come off

Replacing a whole run of base cabinets usually means removing the countertop. Granite and quartz can sometimes be removed and reinstalled, but there's a risk of cracking. Laminate countertops usually can't be reused. That's why drying cabinets in place, and replacing only the damaged panels, is worth trying first when the cabinets are still sound.

Signs of hidden water damage in cabinets

  • Swollen or bubbling cabinet floor under the sink
  • Peeling or lifting edges on cabinet doors or sides
  • A musty smell when you open the cabinet
  • Stains or dark spots inside the cabinet or on the toe kick
  • Vinyl plank or tile near the cabinets lifting or staying damp
  • Swollen baseboards next to the cabinets

How to prevent cabinet water damage

  • Check under your sinks once a month for drips and damp spots.
  • Replace old braided supply lines and corroded shut-off valves.
  • Use a leak alarm or a waterproof cabinet mat under the kitchen sink.
  • Don't store paper goods or cleaners directly on the cabinet floor where a slow leak can hide.
  • Close the valves under the sinks when you leave home for an extended time.

Does insurance cover water-damaged cabinets?

Damage from a sudden leak, such as a burst supply line, is often covered. Damage from a slow drip under the sink that ran for weeks is often excluded as continuous seepage. Matching cabinets and countertops depends on your policy. Photograph everything and keep the failed part. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

Bathroom vanities after a leak

Bathroom vanities have the same weak point as kitchen cabinets: a particle board floor sitting just under the sink plumbing. Toilet supply leaks and overflows also run under vanities and into the wall behind them. Toilet overflows can be Category 2 or Category 3 water, so a vanity soaked by an overflow is often replaced rather than dried. See sewage backup cleanup for toilet backups.

Cabinets in condos

In condos, a leak in a kitchen or bathroom can reach the cabinets in the unit below through the ceiling and walls. Under Florida condo law, the association and owners usually split responsibility depending on what's damaged and what the condo documents say. See condo water damage.

Questions about water-damaged cabinets

Can swollen particle board cabinets be fixed?

Not the swollen part. Particle board doesn't shrink back once it swells. The damaged panel, often just the cabinet floor, can be replaced, or the whole cabinet if it's widely damaged.

How do I know if there's water behind my kitchen cabinets?

Look for swollen toe kicks, a musty smell, peeling edges or damp flooring near the cabinets. A moisture meter on the wall and toe kick, or a thermal camera, confirms it.

Do kitchen cabinets have to be removed after water damage?

Not always. Many cabinets can be dried in place by removing the toe kicks and pushing dry air under and behind them. They're removed when they're badly swollen, contaminated or hiding mold.

Can I save my granite countertop if the cabinets are replaced?

Sometimes. Stone countertops can often be removed and reinstalled, but there's a risk of cracking.

Is a musty smell under the sink a sign of mold?

It can be. A musty smell usually means moisture has been there for a while. Find and fix the leak, then have the cabinet and wall behind it checked.

How long does it take to dry cabinets?

Usually about 3 to 5 days for a clean-water leak caught early, longer if water sat under the cabinets for a while.

Can I paint over water stains inside the cabinet?

Not until the cabinet and the wall behind it are confirmed dry and the leak is fixed. Painting over damp wood or particle board can trap moisture and hide mold.
