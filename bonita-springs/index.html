Imperial River flooding: what to know

  • Flooding can happen without a hurricane. Heavy rain over several days can raise the river.
  • Floodwater is contaminated. Drywall, insulation, carpet and particle board it soaks usually come out.
  • Flood insurance is separate. Standard homeowners policies don't cover rising water.
  • Photograph the water line on the walls before cleanup, for the flood claim.
  • New NFIP flood policies usually take 30 days to start, so buy coverage before storm season.

How water damage restoration works in Bonita Springs homes

  1. Stop the source or wait for floodwater to recede.
  2. Extract standing water with emergency water removal equipment.
  3. Remove contaminated or soaked materials, using flood cuts on drywall when floodwater is involved.
  4. Clean and disinfect after floodwater or sewage.
  5. Dry the structure with dehumidifiers and air movers, logging readings daily. See how structural drying works.
  6. Repair once everything reads dry.

Coastal condos near Bonita Beach

Condo buildings near Bonita Beach and along the coast face both storm surge and the everyday leaks of any multi-unit building: water heaters, AC drains, washers and toilets leaking into units below. Under Florida Statute 718.111(11), the association and owners generally split responsibility depending on what's damaged and what the condo documents say. Report leaks to management right away and photograph everything before cleanup.

Seasonal homes in Bonita Springs

Many Bonita Springs homes in gated and golf communities sit empty for the summer. Before leaving:

  • Shut off the main water and the water heater.
  • Set the AC to control humidity.
  • Have the AC drain line cleared.
  • Arrange regular checks by a home watch service or neighbor, especially after storms.
  • Install leak alarms that alert your phone.

When you return, check the AC, under sinks, the water heater and ceilings, and photograph anything you find with the date you found it.

Older neighborhoods near the river

Does the Imperial River flood homes?

Is Imperial River flooding covered by homeowners insurance?

No. Rising river water is flood damage, covered by a flood policy.

My condo ceiling is leaking from the unit above. What do I do?

How long does it take to dry a home after flooding?

After wet materials are removed and the home is disinfected, drying often takes several days, usually longer than a clean-water leak.
