Water Coming Through Your Ceiling in Fort Myers? Do This Right Now

  • Get people and pets out from under it. A ceiling holding water can drop without warning, and wet drywall is heavy.
  • Turn off power at the breaker for any room where water drips from a light fixture, ceiling fan or smoke detector. Don't touch a wet switch.
  • Stop the source if you can. If your AC is in the attic, switch it off at the thermostat. If a bathroom above is leaking, shut off its water. If it's raining in, the roof can't be stopped from inside, so focus on catching the water.
  • Catch the water. Buckets, towels and plastic sheeting under the drip; move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way.
  • Don't push on a bulge with your hand or stand under it. If a bubble keeps growing and you must relieve it, stand to the side, use a long screwdriver to make one small hole at the lowest point and let it drain into a bucket.
  • Photograph and video the ceiling, the drip and anything damaged before you clean up.

Is a sagging ceiling about to fall?

A flat stain is a warning; a sag or bubble is an emergency. Drywall is gypsum held together by paper. Once it is soaked, it loses strength and the weight of the water pulls it away from the screws.

Treat it as dangerous if you see:

  • A bulge or bubble in the ceiling or the paint
  • Cracks or a sagging line along a drywall seam
  • Screw heads popping through the surface
  • Water dripping from more than one spot
  • A ceiling that feels soft when gently pressed from a ladder at the edge

A sagging section almost always has to be cut out and replaced once the area is dry.

Where is the water coming from?

The location of the stain is your best clue. In Fort Myers, most single-family homes are built on a slab with the AC and many ducts in the attic, so ceiling leaks usually come from above, not from pipes in the floor.

Why ceiling leaks are common in Fort Myers

Attic air handlers. Many Southwest Florida homes have the air handler in the attic, where a clogged condensate line overflows straight onto the ceiling.

Humid attics. Hot, humid attic air causes cold ducts to sweat and drip through ceiling vents.

Afternoon storms and hurricanes. Wind-driven rain gets past roof flashing, vents and soffits, especially after storms that loosen shingles or tiles.

Two-story homes and condos. Bathrooms and laundry rooms above living spaces send leaks straight down.

Plumbing in the attic. Some homes have water lines run through the attic instead of the slab; a pinhole leak there shows up as a ceiling stain.

Why a ceiling stain is more than a paint problem

By the time a stain shows on the ceiling, water has already soaked through the insulation above and the drywall itself. Blown-in and batt insulation hold water like a sponge and keep the drywall wet from above.

In Florida humidity, that hidden moisture rarely dries on its own. Left alone, it leads to:

  • Mold on the back of the drywall and in the insulation, which can start within 24 to 48 hours
  • Stains that come back through fresh paint
  • Sagging and eventual collapse
  • Rot in ceiling joists or roof decking if the leak keeps going

How a wet ceiling is dried or replaced in Fort Myers homes

Find the source and stop it. An HVAC technician, plumber or roofer fixes the cause. A tarp goes on the roof if rain is still getting in.

Measure the moisture. A moisture meter and thermal camera show how far the water spread across the ceiling and down the walls, which is usually wider than the stain.

Decide what stays. A small, recent, clean-water stain can often be dried in place. A ceiling that sagged, stayed wet for days or holds wet insulation above is usually opened up.

Remove wet insulation and damaged drywall. Soaked insulation is bagged out; the wet section of ceiling is cut back to sound drywall.

Dry the framing and attic space. Dehumidifiers and air movers run until the joists and remaining drywall read dry. See how structural drying works.

Repair. New insulation, new drywall, tape, texture and paint, matched to the rest of the ceiling.

Water often runs along joists and down the inside of walls, so expect the walls below the stain to be checked too. If water reached the floor, emergency water removal comes first.

What about popcorn ceilings?

Many older homes in Fort Myers and Cape Coral have textured "popcorn" ceilings. Some textures applied before the mid-1980s contain asbestos. Before a wet popcorn ceiling is scraped or cut, have a sample tested. Don't sand or scrape it yourself.

Does insurance cover a leaking ceiling?

It depends on what caused it:

  • Sudden leaks from a burst supply line, an appliance or an AC overflow are often covered for the resulting damage. See burst pipe cleanup.
  • Roof leaks after a storm may be covered when wind damaged the roof. Hurricane claims usually carry a separate hurricane deductible.
  • Roof leaks from age or wear are often excluded.
  • Slow leaks that ran for weeks are often treated as gradual damage.
  • Leaks from the unit above in a condo involve your HO-6 policy, the neighbor's policy and the association.

Photograph the ceiling before anything is removed, keep the repair invoices that show the cause, and report the date you found the leak truthfully. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

Questions about ceiling leaks

Should I poke a hole in a ceiling bubble?

Only if it keeps growing and you can do it safely: stand to the side, not underneath, use a long screwdriver to make one small hole at the lowest point, and drain it into a bucket. If the ceiling is sagging across a wide area, keep everyone out of the room instead.

Is water dripping from a light fixture dangerous?

Yes. Turn off that circuit at the breaker, don't touch the switch, and keep clear. Have an electrician check the fixture and box before you turn the power back on.

Why is my ceiling leaking when it isn't raining?

Most often it is the AC: a clogged condensate line or sweating ducts in the attic. In two-story homes and condos, it can be a bathroom, laundry or kitchen above.

Can I just paint over a ceiling water stain?

Only after the leak is fixed and the drywall and insulation are confirmed dry with a moisture meter. Paint over wet drywall traps moisture, and the stain usually comes back.

How long does a wet ceiling take to dry?

A small clean-water area often dries in about 3 to 5 days with commercial equipment. If insulation or a large section of drywall has to come out, repairs add more time after drying.

Who fixes the ceiling after the leak is repaired?

The plumber, roofer or HVAC company fixes the source. Drying the ceiling, removing wet insulation and replacing drywall is water damage restoration work.
