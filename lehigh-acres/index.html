24/7 Water Damage Help in Lehigh Acres

Most Lehigh Acres homes are concrete block houses on slabs, so leaks spread under tile and vinyl plank and into walls.

Water damage in Lehigh Acres right now? Do this first

  1. Shut off the water. For homes on a well, turn off the well pump at its breaker as well as the main valve.
  2. Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets or appliances.
  3. Turn off the AC at the thermostat if it's leaking.
  4. Photograph everything before cleanup.
  5. Start drying the same day. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in Florida humidity.

What makes water damage different in Lehigh Acres

Block homes on slabs. Lehigh Acres is largely single-family concrete block homes on slabs, many built in the 2000s and later. Water from a leak runs under flooring and wicks up drywall inside the block walls.

Private wells. A large part of Lehigh Acres draws water from private wells. That means a well pump, a pressure tank, and supply lines that can fail. A leaking pressure tank or line in a garage or utility closet can release water steadily until the pump is shut off.

Drainage during summer storms. Lehigh Acres's canals and drainage are managed by the Lehigh Acres Municipal Services Improvement District (LAMSID). In heavy rain, some streets flood deeply, and new construction on sloped lots can send rainwater straight toward the road and neighboring yards.

AC that rarely rests. Inland Lehigh Acres gets hot, and air conditioners run hard for much of the year. Clogged condensate drain lines are one of the most common causes of indoor water damage in Florida homes.

Families and rentals. Lehigh Acres is one of Lee County's younger communities, with many families and rental homes, where leaks from washers, dishwashers and toilets are common.

How water damage restoration works in Lehigh Acres homes

  1. Stop the source. A plumber, HVAC technician or well service company fixes the cause.
  2. Extract standing water from floors and carpet.
  3. Map the moisture under flooring and inside walls with meters and a thermal camera.
  4. Remove what can't be dried, such as soaked carpet pad, swollen baseboards and cabinet bases.
  5. Dry the structure with dehumidifiers and air movers, logging readings daily. See how structural drying works.
  6. Repair drywall, baseboards, flooring and cabinets once everything reads dry.

Most clean-water leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days. Floodwater and sewage take longer, because contaminated materials have to be removed first.

Well systems and water damage

Homes on private wells have a few extra things to watch:

  • Pressure tanks can rust through at the bottom and leak slowly onto the garage or closet floor.
  • Well pumps keep running when a line bursts, so turn off the pump's breaker along with the main valve.
  • Water treatment equipment, such as softeners and filters, adds fittings that can fail.
  • Low-water periods during droughts can stress pumps and fittings.

Storm flooding in Lehigh Acres

Heavy rain can leave some streets under deep water and turn low lots into islands. When water from outside reaches a garage or the living area, it's floodwater, Category 3, even if it looks clear. Wet drywall, insulation and carpet usually have to be removed before drying, and the damage is covered by a flood policy rather than standard homeowners insurance.

Rental homes: landlords and tenants

Many Lehigh Acres homes are rentals, and water damage in a rental involves two people.

Tenants: stop the water if you safely can, photograph everything and tell your landlord or property manager right away. Your renters insurance usually covers your belongings, not the building.

Landlords and property managers: the building is usually your responsibility. Fast drying protects the property, keeps the home livable and limits mold.

Both: agree on who is calling for drying, and keep copies of photos and messages.

My home is on a well. How do I shut the water off?

Turn off the well pump at its breaker and close the main valve near the pressure tank. That stops the pump from pushing more water into a broken line.

Street flooding got into my garage. Is that covered?

Water rising from outside is flood damage, covered by a flood policy rather than standard homeowners insurance. Photograph everything before cleanup.

My AC closet floor is wet. What should I do?

Turn the AC off at the thermostat, soak up standing water, and check whether the drain pan is full. Then have the drain line cleared and the closet walls and floor checked for hidden moisture.

How long does drying take in a Lehigh Acres home?

Most clean-water leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days. Floodwater, sewage and wet concrete under flooring take longer.

I rent my Lehigh Acres home. Who pays for water damage?

The landlord usually handles the building and its repairs, and renters insurance usually covers your belongings. Tell your landlord right away and photograph everything.

My well pressure tank is leaking into the garage. What do I do?

Does Lehigh Acres flood during hurricanes?

Lehigh Acres is inland, so storm surge is far less of a risk than on the coast. Heavy rain from hurricanes and summer storms can still flood streets, yards and low-lying homes.
