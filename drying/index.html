Water Mitigation and Structural Drying in Fort Myers

Fans running for days and the walls still feel damp? In Fort Myers humidity, wet drywall, slab and cabinets barely dry on their own. Our structural drying uses commercial dehumidifiers, air movers and daily moisture readings to dry your home to a measured standard, so nothing gets closed up wet.

Professional Water Damage Drying in Fort Myers and Lee County

  • Structural drying removes the water you can't see: inside walls, under flooring and in the concrete slab.

Mitigation vs restoration: what's the difference?

Water mitigation stops the damage from getting worse. Restoration puts the house back together. Insurers expect mitigation to start right away; repairs come after the structure is dry.

Water mitigationRestoration
GoalStop further damageRebuild what was damaged
WorkWater removal, tear-out of what can't be saved, drying, antimicrobialDrywall, baseboards, paint, flooring, cabinets
WhenSame day, then 3 to 5 days of drying for most clean-water jobsAfter moisture readings are back to normal

How structural drying works

Map the moisture. Moisture meters and a thermal camera show exactly which walls, floors and cabinets are wet. Dry areas of the same house are measured too, as the target to reach.

Remove what can't dry. Wet carpet pad, soaked insulation, swollen particle board and anything touched by sewage come out. Extraction of standing water happens first through emergency water removal.

Set the equipment. Low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers pull water out of the air; air movers blow across wet walls and floors so moisture evaporates. Desiccant dehumidifiers are used for dense materials like concrete and hardwood.

Dry hidden spaces. Baseboards may come off and small holes are drilled behind them so dry air can reach inside the wall. Cabinet toe kicks come off to dry underneath.

Measure every day. Readings are logged daily and equipment is moved as areas dry. These logs also support your insurance claim.

Confirm it's dry. Drying is finished when wet materials read the same as dry materials of the same type, not when the surface feels dry.

Why drying takes longer in Fort Myers homes

  • Humid outdoor air. Opening windows in Southwest Florida usually adds moisture instead of removing it, so the house is kept closed and the dehumidifiers do the work.
  • Concrete slabs. Most homes sit on a slab, which soaks up water and releases it slowly. Tile and vinyl plank trap it underneath.
  • AC alone isn't enough. Your air conditioner lowers humidity somewhat, but it can't pull water out of walls and slab fast enough to stop swelling and mold.
  • Hidden water from slow leaks. AC condensate overflows and supply-line drips often run for days before anyone notices, so more material is wet by the time drying starts.
  • Block walls. Many Lee County homes have concrete block exterior walls with drywall over furring strips, which can hold moisture behind the drywall.

Why household fans aren't enough

Box fans and a home dehumidifier can help with a small surface spill. After a real leak, they fall short because:

  • They can't reach inside walls, under tile or behind cabinets.
  • A home dehumidifier removes a fraction of what a commercial LGR unit pulls from the air each day.
  • Without readings, nobody knows when the wall or slab is actually dry, and drywall gets closed up wet.
  • Moving damp air around in Florida humidity can spread moisture to dry rooms.

How long does drying take?

Most clean-water jobs dry in about 3 to 5 days with commercial equipment. Dense materials take longer.

The drying classes, in plain English

Drying equipment is sized by how much water got into the structure and how hard the materials are to dry:

ClassWhat it looks like
Class 1A small area; little water absorbed
Class 2A whole room; water wicked up walls and into carpet and pad
Class 3Water came from above; ceilings, walls, insulation and floors soaked
Class 4Dense materials like hardwood, concrete or plaster are wet; needs longer, specialty drying

A higher class means more equipment, more days and a higher cost.

Does drywall have to be cut out?

Not always. Drywall wet with clean water and dried quickly can often stay. It is usually cut out when:

  • The water was sewage, floodwater or another Category 3 source.
  • The insulation behind it is soaked.
  • It sat wet long enough to soften, swell or grow mold.

When it is removed, a "flood cut" is made in a straight line, usually about 2 or 4 feet above the floor, depending on how high the water wicked up.

Drying slabs, floors and cabinets

Slabs dry slowly, and vinyl plank or laminate on top usually has to be lifted so the concrete can breathe. Tile that's still bonded can often stay while the slab dries through the grout. Cabinets are dried by removing the toe kicks and pushing dry air underneath. See water damaged floors and water damaged kitchen cabinets.

Can I turn the fans and dehumidifiers off at night?

Please don't. Drying only works while the equipment runs; turning it off lets humidity climb back and adds days to the job. The equipment is loud and uses power: a large commercial dehumidifier can draw around 10 amps, so keep it on its own circuit when possible and never on an extension cord shared with other appliances.

What drying means for your insurance claim

Most policies expect you to take reasonable steps to stop further damage, and drying is that step. Daily moisture logs, photos and equipment records show the adjuster what was wet and when it was dry. Gradual, long-term leaks are often treated differently from sudden ones. Questions about water mitigation and drying

How do you know the house is dry?

Wet materials are measured against dry materials of the same type in an unaffected part of the house. When they match, drying is finished. Touch and smell are not reliable.

Will I get mold?

Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in humid conditions, which is why drying starts the same day. If mold has already grown, bring in a qualified Florida mold remediator to remove it.

Why are there holes drilled behind my baseboards?

The small holes let dry air flow into the wall cavity, so the inside of the wall can dry without cutting out the drywall. They are covered when the baseboards go back on.

Do I need professional drying if the leak was small?

If the water stayed on a hard surface and was wiped up right away, probably not. If it reached under flooring, into walls or under cabinets, the hidden moisture usually needs commercial equipment and readings.

Can I stay home during drying?

Usually yes after a clean-water leak. Expect noise and warm air around the equipment. Rooms with sewage or floodwater should stay closed off.

Should I open the windows to help it dry?

Usually not in Fort Myers. Humid outdoor air slows drying. Keep the house closed and the AC running unless the crew tells you otherwise.
