A pipe just burst. What do I do?

  1. Shut off the water. Use the valve under the sink or behind the toilet if it's a fixture line. If you can't find it, or the leak is in a wall or ceiling, turn off the main shut-off, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets, cords or appliances.
  3. Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain pressure from the lines.
  4. Move belongings out of the water and lift furniture onto blocks or foil.
  5. Photograph everything before cleanup: the broken pipe or line, the water, wet walls and floors.
  6. Call a plumber to repair the pipe, and start water removal and drying the same day.

Every step in one list: the first-hour water damage checklist.

Where pipes burst in Fort Myers homes

Freezing temperatures are rare in Southwest Florida, so most bursts here come from age, pressure and parts that wear out.

  • Braided supply lines to toilets, sinks and faucets. The rubber inside ages and the line can split without warning.
  • Washing machine hoses, especially original rubber hoses.
  • Water heater connectors and valves in garages, closets and condo utility rooms.
  • Ice maker and refrigerator lines, often thin plastic tubing behind the fridge.
  • Pipes inside walls and attics. Some homes have water lines run through the attic or walls, where a split shows up as a ceiling or wall leak. See ceiling leak help.
  • Pinhole leaks in older copper pipe, which can spray inside a wall for days.
  • Polybutylene pipe, a gray plastic used in many homes built from roughly 1978 to 1995, known for sudden failures.
  • Lines under the slab. Most Fort Myers homes are built on concrete slabs, and a break in a line under the floor is a slab leak. See slab leak water damage.

Signs of a burst pipe you can't see

  • The sound of running water when every fixture is off
  • A water meter that keeps moving with everything turned off
  • A sudden jump in your water bill
  • Wet or bulging drywall, peeling paint or a damp baseboard
  • Warm spots on a tile floor (hot water line)
  • Low water pressure at one or more fixtures
  • A musty smell in one room

How far does the water go?

Supply lines are under pressure, so a burst can release far more water than a drain leak. In a Fort Myers slab home, that water:

  • Spreads under tile, vinyl plank and baseboards across several rooms
  • Soaks the bottom of drywall, which wicks it upward inside the wall
  • Runs under kitchen and bathroom cabinets and swells the particle board bases
  • In a condo, travels down through the ceiling to the unit below. See condo water damage.

That is why the visible puddle is usually only part of the damage.

Burst pipe cleanup and drying, step by step

  1. Plumbing repair. A plumber repairs or replaces the broken section. If the pipe is inside a wall, a section of drywall is opened.
  2. Water extraction. Standing water and water held in carpet are removed with emergency water removal equipment.
  3. Moisture mapping. Meters and a thermal camera find wet areas under flooring and inside walls.
  4. Remove what can't be saved. Carpet pad, swollen baseboards and cabinet bases, and drywall that stayed wet too long.
  5. Structural drying. Dehumidifiers and air movers run around the clock, with readings logged daily. See structural drying.
  6. Repair. Drywall, baseboards, flooring and cabinets are replaced once everything reads dry.

Most burst pipe jobs involve clean water and dry in about 3 to 5 days if drying starts quickly.

Supply line vs drain line: why it matters

Not every leak under a sink is the same.

What it carriesFresh, pressurized waterUsed water and waste

A pressurized supply line keeps leaking until it's shut off, which is why closing the valve is always step one. A leaking drain line under the slab is a different problem; see cast iron pipe water damage.

Is water from a burst pipe clean?

Water from a supply line is Category 1, or clean, water when it's handled fast. But clean water that sits for 24 to 48 hours can turn into Category 2, especially once it soaks into carpet, drywall and cabinet bases. The sooner extraction and drying start, the more can be saved.

Does insurance cover a burst pipe?

Usually yes for the water damage a sudden burst causes: wet drywall, flooring, cabinets and belongings. Common limits:

  • The pipe itself may not be covered, especially if it failed from age or corrosion.
  • Tear-out to reach the pipe is covered under some policies.
  • Slow leaks that ran for weeks are often excluded as gradual.
  • Vacant homes can have limits after a set number of days unoccupied. See water damage in vacant homes.

Photograph everything, keep the plumber's invoice showing what failed, and report the claim promptly. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

How to prevent burst pipes and supply line failures

  • Replace braided supply lines and washer hoses before they age out. Many manufacturers recommend replacing washing machine hoses about every five years.
  • Check your water pressure. Very high pressure strains every line in the house; a plumber can test it and install or adjust a pressure-reducing valve.
  • Know your main shut-off and make sure it turns easily.
  • Install leak sensors under sinks, behind toilets, at the water heater and washer, or an automatic shut-off valve that closes the main when it detects a leak.
  • Turn off the main water when you leave for more than a few days.
  • Replace polybutylene piping if your home still has it.

Questions about burst pipe water damage

What do I do first when a pipe bursts?

Shut off the water at the fixture valve or the main shut-off, cut power to wet rooms at the breaker, and photograph everything. Then call a plumber and start drying the same day.

Why do pipes burst in Florida if it doesn't freeze?

Most bursts here come from aging supply lines and hoses, corroded or pinholed pipes, high water pressure, or failing polybutylene, not freezing.

Can drywall be saved after a burst pipe?

Often yes, if the water was clean and drying starts within a day or two. Drywall that stayed wet longer, swelled or grew mold is usually cut out.

How long does it take to dry after a burst pipe?

Most clean-water burst pipe jobs dry in about 3 to 5 days with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Wet concrete, hardwood and long leaks take longer.

Does homeowners insurance pay for the plumber?

Policies usually pay for the resulting water damage. The repair of the pipe itself may not be covered, though some policies pay to open walls or floors to reach it.

Should I turn off the water heater after shutting off the main?

Yes, if the water will be off for a while. Turn off its breaker, or set a gas heater to pilot or vacation, so the heater isn't running without water coming in.

Where is the main water shut-off in a Fort Myers home?

Usually where the line enters the house: in the garage near the water heater, on an outside wall, or at the meter box near the street, which may need a meter key.

My pipe burst while I was at work. Is it too late?

No, but act now. Shut off the water, photograph everything and start extraction and drying today. The hours since the break matter more than the amount of water you can see.

Pipe burst right now?

Shut off the water, cut power to wet rooms and call. The faster the water comes out, the less has to be replaced.
